Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Ankush Classes handles information when visitors use this educational website. We respect your privacy and strive to maintain transparent and straightforward practices regarding any data shared with us.

2. Information We May Receive

Ankush Classes does not require user accounts or user registration to access study resources. The only personal information we receive is data that you voluntarily submit through our contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The text of your message, resource request, or inquiry

3. Contact Form

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form, your submission is securely transmitted to and processed by Formspree, our third-party form handler. This information is used exclusively to read and respond to your educational question or request. We do not sell, rent, or distribute your email address to commercial marketing lists.

4. Website Usage Information

When you browse our website, our hosting provider (Vercel) automatically logs standard, non-identifying technical metadata (such as IP addresses, browser types, and access timestamps) for security monitoring, server health, and traffic routing purposes. No intrusive tracking or invasive telemetry is operated by Ankush Classes.

5. Cookies

Ankush Classes does not use tracking cookies, targeting cookies, or profiling cookies. Any technical cookies used are strictly functional and essential for site operation or standard browser caching.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes selected third-party services to deliver assets and functionality:

  • Vercel: Static site hosting and content delivery network.
  • Formspree: Contact form delivery.
  • Google Fonts & FontAwesome CDN: Web typography and icon delivery.
  • YouTube: Embedded or linked video resources from the official channel.

These external services maintain their own privacy practices and terms of service.

7. Study Materials & External Links

Study materials, notes, or sample papers may be hosted directly on the site or linked from external storage locations. Ankush Classes is not responsible for the privacy practices or contents of third-party external websites linked from our pages.

8. Children's Privacy

Ankush Classes provides study resources intended for secondary and senior secondary students (Classes 8 through 12). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under applicable age thresholds without parental consent. If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal details through our contact form without permission, please contact us for immediate deletion.

9. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures, including HTTPS encryption across all web pages, to protect the transmission of inquiries. However, no data transmission over the internet can be guaranteed as completely invulnerable, and visitors submit messages at their own discretion.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in website functionality or legal standards. Updated versions will be published directly on this page with an updated revision date.
