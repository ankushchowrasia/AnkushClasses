Platform Background

About Ankush Classes

Ankush Classes is an educational platform created by Ankush to provide clear, useful and accessible learning resources for students from Class 8 to Class 12.

The platform was founded on the belief that exam preparation and conceptual understanding improve significantly when students have access to well-organized notes, focused question papers, and reliable subject explanations.

What We Provide

Subject-Focused Notes

Structured chapter summaries, key points, and revision notes tailored for quick understanding.

Question & Sample Papers

Exam-oriented questions, past papers, and model answers to help benchmark progress.

Humanities & English

In-depth materials across History, Sociology, Political Science, Geography, Civics, and English.

Law Basics & Awareness

Introductory concepts explaining legal frameworks, fundamental rights, and constitutional principles.

Classes & Boards

Ankush Classes currently focuses on students from Classes 8 to 12, with dedicated resources and subject notes designed for ICSE, ISC, and CBSE curricula.

ICSE

Classes 8–10 foundation and board resources.

ISC

Classes 11–12 Humanities and Arts stream specialization.

CBSE

Classes 8–12 foundational and high school subject support.

Our Approach

The goal is simple: make learning resources easier to find, easier to understand and easier to use during preparation.

Rather than creating complicated barriers, Ankush Classes organizes study materials directly by subject and class. Every resource is curated to give students clarity, save time during revision, and build confidence ahead of examinations.
